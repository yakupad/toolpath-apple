Support

Found a bug, a G-code file Toolpath reads wrong, or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. Attaching the G-code file (or the part that causes trouble) helps a lot.

Common questions

Are the feeds and speeds safe to use as-is?
They are conservative starting points to test, not guarantees. Always air-cut new files, check your workholding and wear eye and ear protection.

Why does the whole toolpath look outside my machine?
G-code coordinates are usually relative to your work zero (G54), not the machine's home. The travel check is off by default for this reason.

How does sync work?
Through your own private iCloud. Make sure iCloud is turned on for Toolpath in Settings. There is no separate account.

Can Toolpath control my machine?
No. The live monitor is read-only: it only asks the controller for its status.

Restoring purchases
Settings → Toolpath Pro → Restore Purchases.
