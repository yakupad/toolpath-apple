Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Toolpath is built so that your data stays yours. The developer does not collect any personal data.

No account

Toolpath has no sign-up and no login.

Your data stays on your devices and in your iCloud

G-code files, machines, tools, recipes, job history, stock and maintenance records are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on, they sync through your own private iCloud database, which only you can access. The developer cannot see it.

Analysis happens on your device

G-code parsing, safety checks, feeds and speeds and all calculators run locally.

On-device assistant

The assistant uses Apple Intelligence on your device. Your questions are not sent to the developer.

Live machine monitor (local network)

If you use the optional live monitor, Toolpath connects to the CNC controller address you enter on your local network and only reads its status. Nothing from this connection is sent anywhere else.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. The developer receives no payment details.

Analytics and tracking

Toolpath contains no analytics or advertising SDKs, does not track you and does not share data with advertisers or data brokers. Apple may give the developer aggregated, anonymous crash and usage statistics if you chose to share them with app developers in your device settings.

Children

Toolpath is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.

Türkçe özet

Toolpath hiçbir kişisel veri toplamaz. Hesap yoktur. Verileriniz cihazınızda ve yalnızca sizin erişebildiğiniz özel iCloud veritabanınızda tutulur. Analizler ve asistan cihaz üzerinde çalışır. Reklam ve analiz SDK'sı yoktur. Satın alımları Apple yönetir.
